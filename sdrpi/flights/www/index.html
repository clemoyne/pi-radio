Takeoffs and landings are worked out from each track: it starts or ends below 1,500 ft within 8 km of an airport with scheduled service, then climbs or after descending at least 1,000 ft. Airports far away are only counted when the receiver hears aircraft low there, and small airfields without scheduled service aren't counted. "Overhead" uses the closest point of each flight path to the house.
