Messages

Newest first, up to 300. ACARS is the text link between an airliner and its airline: gate and fuel reports, weather, maintenance data, and sometimes air traffic control messages. Much of it is coded by each airline, so the raw text is shown as received, with the parts that have a standard meaning explained.

Received only, never transmitted. Listening is fine in Canada, but the law bars sharing or using what you intercept.
